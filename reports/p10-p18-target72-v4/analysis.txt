What was trained and what benefit is supported

The frozen corpus contains 754 public exchanges from 236 sessions across 12 cases.
The selected predictors use 252 P10/P18 exchanges from 106 sessions. Experiments
included all-case histories plus dated purchasing, supplier, contract and index
records. Broader-history variants did not pass selection. P10 selected tactical
email/evidence features plus business context. Its historical test next-quote MSE
fell about 33.4% and MAE about 31.3% against its incumbent. This is prediction error,
not a negotiation-score improvement. P18 retained its refitted baseline: the
tactical candidate reduced MSE but increased MAE and was rejected by the gate.

These are response estimators and historical-experience retrieval, not a fine-tune
of Claude's language-model weights. Hard execution controls remain independent.
The hosted sample does not demonstrate an outcome benefit from learned advice.
The baseline/advisory development comparison is too small, and strategies vary.

Keyword cues and concrete actions

case-scoped records. It retains source IDs, dates, hashes and exact excerpts;
marks shortened/deferred excerpts; excludes future, unrelated or undated sources;
and labels buyer estimates. No keyword is treated as a proven causal lever.

Why P18 did not close, and what changed in the last run

In both frozen validations, menus ultimately produced a binding EUR 5.20/90-day
offer and an advertised EUR 5.10/30-day alternative. The price cap was EUR 5.10,
so the standing offer was not eligible. The cheaper alternative was not yet
server-acceptable. Even one unchanged confirmation after 'final offer' caused
withdrawal. Knowing the response pattern does not make a non-standing ID binding.

The final diagnostic kept one 30-day, 36-month package throughout. Buyer proposals
were EUR 4.60 with 0% deadband, then EUR 5.00 with 5% deadband and the verified PPI
argument, then EUR 5.04 with the same clause and the verified AS argument. Supplier
quotes moved 6.10 -> 5.40 -> 5.20 -> 5.10. This time EUR 5.10 was the standing
final offer, and the controller immediately accepted its exact eligible ID.

This supports resolving package ambiguity before finality. It does not isolate
whether keywords, concession size, package count or wording caused the result.
It also shows that verified evidence can be acknowledged without moving the
supplier below EUR 5.10. Repeated traces do not reveal a proven private floor.

Implementation and practical limit

The source-linked cues are now part of ResponseSupport alongside business context,
retrieved historical proposals/responses and optional forecasts. They remain
internal audit data for ordinary shadow buyers; explicit experimental advice can
use them. The original prompt input stays unchanged in shadow mode. Evidence,
package costs, current acceptance eligibility and saved limits retain authority.
Automatic cue integration was added after the twelve runs and verified offline;
there is no further hosted result for that integration. The new v4 weights remain
separate because this experiment did not demonstrate better negotiation outcomes.
