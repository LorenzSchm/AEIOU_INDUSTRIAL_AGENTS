How to use
Replace the evaluation prompt with prompts/p18-diagnostic-v6.txt. Do not append it to V5 or an always-close strategy. Run P18 only. opening-request.txt is the supplier-facing body; request-fields.json contains its transport fields. The model still needs to produce the current assessment and numeric_claims specified in V6.
The application research loader supplies the detailed evidence automatically when P18 starts.
