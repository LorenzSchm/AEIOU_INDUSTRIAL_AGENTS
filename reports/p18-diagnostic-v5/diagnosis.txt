P18: Why the 25.7-point offer was accepted, and the new 80-point floor

Observed run: b8abc5e1bc524b819fd33a7279826f9c.
The supplier's final binding terms were 5.60 EUR/kg, 14 days, 36 months,
AS-14404 / 45% / no deadband / base 2026-08. Total cost: 276514.75 EUR.
Official score: 25.7280415 points.

The target proposal at 4.45 cost 219730.47 EUR. The supplier never accepted that
price. It kept its own 5.60 while taking the other conditions. The buyer's next
decision explicitly invoked the unconditional Final closing rule and accepted
the only eligible live offer. Eligibility meant a valid standing offer cheaper
than the alternative; it did not mean a score of 80 or more.

V4 contained: 'The target is not a veto on the original closing rule.' It also
required accepting on Final, agreement or exhausted sends even above target.
The new user instruction explicitly supersedes those rules. An 80-point
acceptance boundary was not implemented in V4.

Exact new threshold
Score = 100 * (295173.23 - C_deal) / (295173.23 - 222651.27).
Score >= 80 requires C_deal <= 237155.662 EUR.
Since evaluated costs are rounded to cents, the allowed maximum is 237155.66.
237155.67 is below 80 even though a display could round its score to 80.0.

Changes
prompts/p18-diagnostic-v5.txt is a COMPLETE replacement, not an appended patch.
It contains a structured acceptance_floor block that activates the code guard.
It removes unconditional acceptance and the mandatory 5.18 opening, which
itself scored only 70.707 on the old terms. The first proposal is now the target
exchange directly. The existing shrinking, reciprocal concession discipline
remains. An 80-point package is the last acceptable boundary; near 100 remains
the objective.

The controller derives the exact maximum cost from the configured benchmark,
checks the case ID and actual opening cost, and filters the model's eligible
offers. Acceptance of a package above the boundary is rejected. If a model
insists after its validation retry, execution fails without sending acceptance.
A second check immediately before the hosted send prevents bypassing the
controller. Term-sheet costs include payment and index terms.

The new stop action terminates locally without acceptance. The evaluation's
existing cleanup can close the remote session without a deal. That is not a
successful negotiation and is never assigned an invented 80-point score.
If a useful legal move remains, V5 continues negotiation. Final alone does not
waive the minimum. When no acceptable offer or useful legal move remains,
V5 stops rather than accepting below 80.

Scope
The structured policy is explicit and case-scoped. V5 activates it for P18.
Unconfigured earlier prompts retain their behavior for reproducible comparison;
replace V4 with the entire V5 file, including the policy block. A different case
or a different opening benchmark fails validation rather than borrowing P18's
threshold. No benchmark for an unknown case is invented.
