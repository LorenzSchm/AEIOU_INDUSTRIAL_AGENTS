B) €27.50 per unit | 23,400 pcs/year | 36 months | 42 days lead time

Payment terms remain 30 days net and DAP, as in the enquiry.

Our reasoning on price:
- Our current price is €29.89, and the last two renewals on this part were settled at 0%.

- In return, you receive a firm, multi-year full-volume commitment. You can plan capacity against it instead of allocating.

If it helps your capacity planning, we are also happy to align on an even monthly call-off schedule.

Could you let us know which package works for you, or which term you would need adjusted?

Kind regards,
Purchasing
