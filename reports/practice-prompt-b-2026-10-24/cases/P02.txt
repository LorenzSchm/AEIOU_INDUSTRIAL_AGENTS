Subject: Re: Angebot part:350011 – Motor IE3 7.5 kW from 01.01.2027

BEGIN VERBATIM BODY

Dear Ms Lechner,

Thank you for your Angebot and for setting out your cost structure so clearly.

We recognise the copper increase. CU-NOTATION is up 47.7% year on year, and we take that seriously. We also want to acknowledge your delivery performance: on-time delivery has improved from about 81% to over 90% this year, which matters a lot to us. Our aim is a price that keeps this supply healthy and stable for 2027.

At the same time, a 6.5% step on a 12-month fixed price is more than we can support:
- Part of the copper rise already fell into 2026, when we settled at +1.2%.
- Your other drivers are moderate: LCI +2.7% and PPI +6.5% on smaller cost shares.
- The main remaining risk for you is further copper movement, and that can be handled with a clause rather than built into the base price.

We therefore propose three options. Each covers 6,110 pcs/year, a 12-month term, 35 days lead time and DAP:

A) €356.00 per unit, Zahlungsziel 30 days net, no Preisgleitklausel
B) €353.50 per unit, Zahlungsziel 30 days net, CU-NOTATION clause (20% material share, 5% deadband, base month 2026-08)
C) €354.00 per unit, Zahlungsziel 14 days net, no Preisgleitklausel

Option B protects you against a further copper rise. Option C brings your cash in more than two weeks earlier.

Could you let us know which structure works best for Nordmark, or which adjustment within these packages would make it work? We are happy to align quickly so this can go to your pricing committee in its next session.

Kind regards,
Purchasing
Hollerau Pumpenwerke GmbH

END VERBATIM BODY
