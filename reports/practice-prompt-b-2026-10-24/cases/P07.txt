Thank you for your Angebot for the complete fastener kit (part:360010, 360011, 360012, 360030 – 30,000 kits per year, DAP, 5 days Lieferzeit).

We propose three packages, all DAP, 5 days lead time, 30,000 kits per year. Please choose the one that suits your cash flow best:

A) €4.63 per kit – 90 days net – 24 months
B) €4.60 per kit – 60 days net – 24 months
C) €4.55 per kit – 14 days net – 24 months

If a longer commitment would help you plan, for example restarting your second shift, we are also open to discussing 36 months against a further price step. We are happy to share a rolling forecast and a fixed call-off rhythm.

We understand that a change of this size needs your pricing committee. Could you table it at the next session and let us know which package works for you, or how you would adjust it?

3) Cost drivers: We have looked at PPI-C2594 and LCI-C. Year on year they rose by about 1.7% and 2.7%. Weighted for a fastener kit, that is a little over 1%, which does not explain the gap between your offer and the price level a firm, single-source volume of this size should reach.

We have moved towards you. Here are three revised packages, all DAP, 5 days Lieferzeit, 30,000 kits per year:

A) €4.73 per kit – 90 days net – 24 months
B) €4.65 per kit – 14 days net – 24 months
C) €4.68 per kit – 90 days net – 36 months (longer volume security for you)

If faster payment helps your cash flow, B is the natural choice. If planning security matters most, C gives you 36 months of guaranteed kit volume.

We understand this needs your pricing committee. Could you table it at the next session and let us know which package works for you, or how you would adjust it?
