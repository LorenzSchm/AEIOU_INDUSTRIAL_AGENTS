We prefer the 5% deadband that you proposed yourself. It keeps adjustments to real movements and reduces administration on both sides.

| C | €5.50/kg | 60 days | none (fixed for 24 months) |

- **Package B** gives you faster cash flow.
- **Package C** is for the case where you would rather offer a fixed price after all.
