Opening assessment
More facts in the first email is not an established fix. Runs
0fb5e14c8e7b408480f8e4dc51aae482 and 54747c61df044ac09c5e084e596da97f already
combined PO, PPI and AS evidence in the opening and requested committee/owner
approval. The facts verified, but those runs stalled at EUR 5.20/5.30 packages
and withdrew after low counters and later requests. Those trials also changed
price, payment and concession timing, so they cannot isolate the effect of the
opening. Likewise, the latest improved result does not prove which change caused it.
V20 preserves the latest run's PO -> PPI -> AS sequence and EUR 4.60 anchor. It
shortens the opening and requests the approval route early. Its main new test is
the 45-day compromise after the menu while the supplier remains open.
